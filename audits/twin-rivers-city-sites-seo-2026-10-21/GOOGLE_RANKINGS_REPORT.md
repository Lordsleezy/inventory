## 3. Indexation notes

- `site:domain` operator queries often returned **empty** via WebSearch — treated as **tool limitation**, not proof of deindexation (contradicted by keyword SERPs and branded domain hits).

### Multi-city power player

**Fantastic Fence (callfantasticfence.com)**  
- Physical offices: Rocklin, Sacramento, Elk Grove  
- License #979657, heavy review claims (1,000+), city landing pages for each market  
- **Why they win:** Real multi-location NAP + review density + dedicated city URLs on one strong domain  

### Rocklin / Placer

- **Cal-Sierra Fence** — Rocklin street address, 20+ years, broad county coverage  
- **rocklinfence.com** — Aggressive city keyword site (lead-gen pattern)

### Roseville

- **rosevillefencecompany.com** — Clean branded domain, strong head-term presence  
- **Fence Lifts** — Repair-focused, street address in Roseville, strong reviews narrative  
- **Linmoore** — Fabrication/iron specialist landing pages  

### Folsom / El Dorado corridor

- **Stronghold Fencing** — HOA-focused Folsom content  
- **folsomfencecompany.com** — Local address claim (Bidwell St)  
- **GreenTech Builders** — Redwood/iron niche  

### Elk Grove

- **Build Rite** — Elk Grove based, strong local trust signals  
- **elkgrovefencecompany.com** — Marketing site pattern  
- Fantastic Fence EG office page  

### Grass Valley / Nevada County

- **D-Fence** — Strong reviews narrative, local address  
- **Sta-Bull Fence** — Owner-on-every-job positioning since 1982  
- **Nevada County Fence**, **Gold Country Fence** — Long tenure + local phone (530)  

### Exploitable competitor weaknesses

1. Many competitors thin on **specific HOA subdivision guidance**.  
2. Few publish genuine **permit + soil** depth Twin Rivers already drafts.  
3. Lead-gen city sites (generic copy) can be beaten with project photos + license verification + GBP.  
4. Twin Rivers can win **repair + storm** and **gate** niches with faster response content + call tracking.  
5. Do **not** invent backlink metrics — pursue citations: CSLB, chamber, supplier pages, local news, sponsorships (evidence-based only).
